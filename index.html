OpenGraph embeds work by adding specific <meta> tags to the <head> of your HTML document. When a link to your page is shared on platforms that support OpenGraph, these platforms scrape your page for these meta tags (such as og:title, og:image, og:description). They then use this metadata to generate a rich preview card with an image, title, and description, making the shared link more engaging and informative than a simple text URL.
